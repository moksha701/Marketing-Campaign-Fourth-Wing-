# Survive the Parapet — Fourth Wing Offline Activation Campaign

## 📌 Project Overview

A conceptual integrated offline activation campaign developed for Amazon MGM Studios 
and The Fourth Wing TV adaptation. This deck proposes a real-world experiential 
marketing activation inspired by the most iconic scene in Rebecca Yarros's bestselling 
novel — the parapet crossing at Basgiath War College — brought to life in New York City.

This is a self-initiated conceptual project created to demonstrate campaign strategy, 
experiential marketing planning, and data-informed budget development.

---

## 💡 The Big Idea

In the world of Fourth Wing, every first-year student at a military war college must 
cross the parapet. A narrow, crumbling stone walkway suspended hundreds of feet in the 
air. No safety net. No second chances.

We bring that moment to New York City. A safe, padded, supervised parapet structure 
installed at Flatiron Plaza. Passersby attempt the crossing, feel the thrill, and walk 
away wanting more. Every attempt gets recorded and shared on Instagram and Prime Video. 
The activation markets itself.
